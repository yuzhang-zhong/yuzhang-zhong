## Research

I study **how AI agents behave and how people can understand and correct them**. I'm interested in the decisions agents make during a task, what changes across different runs, and how people can inspect and revise an agent's model of their intentions and choices.

- **[TraceFork](https://yuzhang-zhong.github.io/publications/) · EMNLP 2026 Demo**  
  Aligning and comparing GUI-agent trajectories to understand where their behavior diverges and where human intervention may help.
- **[RewindAI](https://yuzhang-zhong.github.io/portfolio/rewindai) · UIST 2026 Poster**  
  Inspectable, revisable autobiographical AI clones that help people reflect on social decisions and explore paths they did not take.
- **[SNS-Grasp](https://yuzhang-zhong.github.io/publication/2026-sns-grasp) · AAAI 2026**  
  Using semantic information to generate robotic grasps that better match what a person intends to do.
